You sat at your desk all day. So why does it feel like you studied nothing?

In one line A day at the desk is not a day of study. When every 10 minutes is coloured by the subject you actually studied, you finally see where the day went, and you can fix it.

You sat down at 8 in the morning and got up at 10 at night. It felt like a full day. But between the chai breaks, the phone, the doubt you searched on YouTube and the chat that took "only two minutes", how much of it was study?

This isn't about blaming yourself. Almost everyone remembers the hours they sat, not the hours they studied. The problem is that without a real number you can't see where the day went, so tomorrow looks exactly the same.

Padhai Timer counts only while you study, and only for the subject you tapped. Every 10-minute box in the planner gets that subject's colour. At night you see it plainly: three blue hours of Physics, one orange hour of Chemistry, and a white gap after lunch.

That white gap is not a failure. It's information. Once you see it, you know exactly which hour to protect tomorrow, and which subject you keep avoiding.
