Installing takes 10 seconds. And it's safe.

Many people worry about installing apps from a link. Here is exactly what happens.

  • Why not the Play Store?Padhai Timer is a web app. 'Install' / 'Add to Home Screen' is an official feature built into Chrome (Google) and Safari (Apple), so no store is needed in between.
  • It is not an APK fileNothing is downloaded from an unknown link. Your own Chrome or Safari adds it to the home screen, the same way it saves a website.
  • No permissionsIt does not ask for your contacts, photos, location, camera or microphone.
  • Almost no spaceLess than 1 MB. It works well on basic phones and slow internet.
  • Remove it any timePress and hold the icon, then tap Remove. That's it.
  • No account neededYou don't type a phone number, email or password to start.

  • Live study hallWho is studying your exam right now, and today's ranking.
  • Study groupsUp to 5 groups. Share a 6-letter code or a WhatsApp invite.
  • 10-minute plannerEach 10-minute box takes the colour of the subject you studied.

Safe for students

  • No phone number, no email neededYou get an anonymous account automatically. Google sign-in is optional, only to keep your log on a new phone.
  • Others see very littleOnly your nickname, exam and today's study time. Never your full log.
